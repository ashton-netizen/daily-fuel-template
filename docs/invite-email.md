# An email you can send

For anyone you want to share Daily Fuel with. Replace the three placeholders. Send it from your own mail; if it contains the link, a plain
link in the body is best (no tracking, no wrapper).

---

**Subject:** A morning routine you can build in an afternoon

Hi <name>,

You asked how I get clear each morning. It is a private page that walks me through yesterday's lessons, my beliefs and gratitude, my purpose
and my summit, then ranks my best move for the day from my rocks, calendar and inbox. It is built in my own Claude account, and nothing leaves it.

You can have your own in about twenty minutes. It is a template: Claude interviews you with ten questions (your purpose, beliefs, goals, rocks, people), then
builds and publishes your private version. None of my details are in it.

1. Open <template link> and click **Use this template** (make it private), then download the folder.
2. Open the folder in the Claude desktop app (the Code tab).
3. Paste the prompt in `setup/master-prompt.md` and answer the questions.

The README has the details. You will need the Claude desktop app on a plan that includes artifacts; Gmail and Calendar are optional.

<call-out line, your own words>

<sign-off>
